# F. Technical priors, SNP ascertainment and coordinate provenance

## Decision

Prioritize a provenance and SNP-ascertainment audit over external masking. The available evidence does not establish that a low AT-to-depth ratio measures technical dropout, and low heterozygous-SNP density could be biological, technical, or selection-related. No quantitative region-level mechanism is identified. MCF10-A is a non-malignant development sample, not a region-level truth set.

## What the local audit establishes

The [phased VCF](/home/mami/Desktop/EGA/SNP_project/alpha/haplo_initial/updated_phasing/phasing/phased.vcf.gz) header records chr1 length 248,956,422 and an Eagle 2.4.1 command with hg38-labelled 1000 Genomes reference and hg38 map. This strongly supports hg38 for the phasing step; it does **not** prove the identity, patch, contig set or checksum of the alignment FASTA or that all inputs use it. No overlap with external tracks should be interpreted until alignment and annotation assemblies are checked.

The [builder](/home/mami/Desktop/EGA/haplotype_aware_cnv/Build_Aligned_HA_HB.py:193) retains phased `0|1` and `1|0` calls, matches pileup sites by chromosome, position, REF and ALT, maps VCF positions and 1-based inclusive bins to 0-based half-open intervals, and sums per-site DP into bins. HA+HB equals selected-site DP by construction. It does not retain phase-set metadata; the inspected VCF records show GT-only FORMAT, so independently supported global orientation is not demonstrated. Reference-panel phasing does not by itself prove error-free chromosome-wide phase or sample heterozygosity.

[CellSNP documentation](https://cellsnp-lite.readthedocs.io/en/latest/main/manual.html) defines DP as REF+ALT and AD as ALT, and supports read or UMI counts depending on invocation. AT is a sum of SNP-site observations: one underlying molecule may cover more than one selected site. The actual pileup tool version, UMI mode, read filters, SNP list and genotype-calling thresholds are **unknown**; do not apply documented defaults retrospectively. Depth and AT may have different units and are dependent measurements. The builder defaults to updated-phasing inputs and the target output directory ([lines 267–291](/home/mami/Desktop/EGA/haplotype_aware_cnv/Build_Aligned_HA_HB.py:267)); the [design](/home/mami/Desktop/EGA/haplotype_aware_cnv/docs/plans/2026-03-16-aligned-ha-hb-design.md:7) and output auxiliary filenames support this linkage. Older reports explicitly describe a distinct 18,738-bin SNP-filtered matrix. This is strong contextual correspondence, not proof that the current file revision produced these exact outputs: the historical execution and script revision remain unverified. Inspected script SHA-256: `b3892252f9198f14539d2a16be19b9a8fa8e346c2dc348731366f5cbb56475df`. See [pilot.md](pilot.md) for exact line locators and header excerpt.

The [local pyEpi code](/home/mami/Desktop/EGA/pyEpiAneufinder/pyEpiAneufinder/pyEpiAneufinder_v1.py) supports blacklist window exclusion and a configurable cohort-zero filter (default 0.85); [published methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10511508/) describe both and GC correction. The retained 25,337-bin grid alone cannot reveal excluded-bin signals or independently determine each exclusion reason. Gap-aware span and callable-bin counts must accompany region comparisons.

## Rejected shortcuts and next experiment

Do not hard-mask every sparse or low-SNP bin, divide AT by raw depth as a probability without checking units, assume an observed allele balance excludes LOH, or impose global haplotype orientation. Population reference SNP density cannot replace sample-heterozygote ascertainment. No pretrained cell-type atlas is justified without compatible inputs.

Next, recover the actual alignment reference manifest, cellsnp and genotype/phasing commands and pyEpi run config. On the frozen shared cells and fixed regions, summarize raw depth and AT separately per cell and retained callable span, alongside selected site counts, DP distribution and missing genomic gaps; compare matched regions. Decision criterion: only consider a targeted technical prior if the regional deficit persists after explicit denominator/ascertainment checks and a prespecified sensitivity analysis shows stable event interpretation under flagged versus unflagged bins. This is an exploratory diagnostic, not validation or proof of mechanism.
