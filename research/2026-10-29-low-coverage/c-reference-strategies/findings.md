# C. Reference strategies: decision and scope

**Decision:** no reliable normal or diploid reference has been identified for the current MCF10-A sample. Proceed with explicitly relative, baseline-sensitive exploratory summaries and abstain from absolute copy-state or normal-cell claims. Non-malignant identity and a WNN cluster are not regional DNA truth. A consistent dip shared across cells can reflect a shared event or shared technical/accessibility bias.

| Screen | Role and decision |
|---|---|
| Independently supported internal controls | First choice when present and compatible; validate identity and regional DNA separately, never infer normality from cell type alone. |
| Compatible external scATAC normals | Second choice with explicit donor, cell-type and technical mismatch checks; no universally suitable public panel established here. |
| Self-centered reference | Useful relative sensitivity diagnostic in homogeneous samples; cannot anchor absolute ploidy or recover a whole-genome shift. The published epiAneufinder baseline uses the cell genome-wide mean ([paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10511508/)). |
| Signac / ArchR transfer | Optional cell-type hypothesis, not a malignancy or diploidy test; inputs not located. [Signac](https://stuartlab.org/signac/articles/integrate_atac) transfers labels using matched chromatin data; [ArchR](https://www.archrproject.com/archive/1.0.1/reference/addGeneIntegrationMatrix.html) integrates a gene-score matrix with scRNA and reports predicted labels and scores. |
| CN-derived normals | Sensitivity comparator only: [CopyscAT](https://github.com/spcdot/CopyscAT/blob/master/copyscat_tutorial.R) offers inferred or supplied controls; [CopyKAT](https://github.com/navinlabcode/copykat) and [inferCNV](https://github.com/broadinstitute/infercnv/wiki/Interpreting-the-figure) are RNA methods. Reusing focal CN signal for selection and testing makes apparent normality circular. |
| DNA panel of normals | Technical analogy, not an interchangeable scATAC panel: [GDC](https://docs.gdc.cancer.gov/Data/Bioinformatics_Pipelines/DNA_Seq_WGS/) uses DNA controls to denoise DNA counts; accessibility introduces additional cell-type effects. |

## Decision tree and acceptance checks

1. **Independent control available?** Verify provenance, independently characterized CN at the regions of interest, cell identity, assembly, bin geometry, counting units, library preparation and sufficient coverage. In mixed tissue, annotation supports identity but does not establish malignancy or diploidy. Compare coverage-matched internal controls across donors/cell types, hold out controls and evaluate concordance. If the checks pass, use an internal reference with uncertainty and document excluded cells. Controls may themselves carry shared germline or somatic CN changes.
2. **Otherwise, suitable external scATAC controls?** Assess donor, cell type/state, sex chromosome context, library preparation, sequencing chemistry, processing, genome build, accessibility and depth. Verify legal access and data license and leave out donors in sensitivity checks. If mismatch dominates or controls lack independent characterization, do not label the panel reliable. In particular, a public peak atlas or DNA PoN is not automatically a copy-number normal panel.
3. **Otherwise, unanchored analysis:** report relative comparisons under several prespecified plausible baseline definitions, covariate and region exclusions. Check sensitivity to removing candidate regions and to coverage strata; do not optimize the baseline against desired calls. Shared broad events, extensive aneuploidy, uneven accessibility, or changes across most bins may corrupt every self-baseline. In a homogeneous line, this branch is often the only currently feasible one. If stability or assumptions fail, return **no reliable reference identified**, with affected calls unresolved. Even a stable relative profile does not establish absolute ploidy; an independent DNA anchor is needed.

## Three options in depth

**Independent matched internal controls.** Aggregate normal control cells only after independent evidence and appropriate QC; check that each region has enough callable observations and compare controls from independent specimens where possible. A cell-type label and high transfer score are not proof of normal CN: lineage, malignancy and copy state are distinct. Detection of a group with low CN variation using the same regions under test creates selection bias; cross-fitting or exclusion of the focal region may reduce direct reuse but cannot prove normality or reveal a clonal event shared by every candidate. No currently identified internal controls meet these criteria.

**External matched references and Numbat-multiome.** The [2025 Numbat-multiome paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12531993/) evaluates direct scATAC ATAC-bin inference against WGS and constructs normalized bin-by-cell-type reference counts from normal cells, including healthy donor plasma cells in one cohort and normal B/other immune cells in another. Its [vignette](https://kharchenkolab.github.io/numbat/articles/numbat-multiome.html) specifies raw bin-level integer fragment counts, a normalized feature reference, genomic annotation and phased SNP-level observations. It also describes separate same-sample RNA/ATAC combined mode; same-nucleus 10x multiome is not fully supported. A matching reference is essential to its published workflow; the source does not establish no-normal performance here. The official [package metadata](https://github.com/kharchenkolab/numbat/blob/main/DESCRIPTION) reports 1.5.2 and MIT; pin the actual commit before use. Cross-sample differences in accessibility can mimic dosage, and control CN alterations can be subtracted. Current bin raw-count units, SNP-level inputs, phase sets and compatible normal reference remain unverified; AT totals are SNP-site DP, not established unique fragment coverage.

**Reference-free sensitivity.** Compare existing per-cell genome-centered relative depth with independently prespecified robust regional baselines or matched-region contrasts, using raw counts and coverage offsets separately from processed depth. This is an untested design, not a validated absolute CN method. Document which bins were retained, genomic gaps and baseline regions; assess whether the candidate is stable across alternatives and evaluate against orthogonal DNA. Neither robust centering nor apparent majority state proves a diploid anchor. The chosen control regions must not be assumed normal; region selection and evaluation require separate data or calibration.

## Specific public accessibility reference candidate

The [Zhang et al. adult human sci-ATAC atlas](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8664161/) profiles 30 adult tissues with cell-type annotations from multiple primary tissue donors; donor eligibility excluded metastatic cancer and recent chemotherapy, but that does not establish copy-neutral genomes at each locus. Its generated datasets are publicly available via GEO, with individual sample-level fragment BEDs and SRA records; [GSM5589349](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM5589349) is a concrete aorta example. This is a candidate for assessing cross-cell-type accessibility and building a reference from compatible fragment counts, **not an independently DNA-validated panel of normals**. Pick tissue and cell types based on the future sample, rather than forcing an MCF10-A match.

Practical constraints: the atlas uses sci-ATAC rather than necessarily the query chemistry; donor number, tissue-cell coverage, biological state and batch may not represent the query. Adult analysis used GRCh38; the integrated fetal component was lifted over from hg19, so select and verify the individual sample and exact assembly, reference contigs and coordinate conventions. Fragment files must retain usable barcodes and cell annotations and be rebinned with the same counting rules and retained intervals; confirm these and per-dataset access/reuse terms before use. Peaks, integrated embeddings, smoothed profiles or normalized peak matrices cannot simply stand in for raw whole-genome bin counts. A normal Numbat ATAC-bin reference additionally requires an independently defensible normal-cell reference and compatible normalized bin counts; neither is established by atlas membership. Check donor-level CN against independent DNA, compare held-out donors and measure cross-chemistry and cell-type deviations before adopting any candidate. No atlas files were downloaded.

## Missing evidence and next experiment

Do not treat zero allele observations as balance or evidence for 0:0; low counts, ascertainment, phase switches and mapping bias limit inference. Depth and AT may reuse molecules and are not independent confirmation. Wave 1 found very sparse regional AT, unknown phase continuity and unresolved alignment-reference provenance ([shared brief](../shared/WAVE1_BRIEF.md)); consult E and F for details. The RNA-only characterization in E's first-pass Numbat allele discussion does not apply to the later published scATAC mode.

**Next experiment:** obtain independently characterized, protocol-compatible scATAC benchmark samples spanning matched controls, homogeneous no-normal samples and high-CN-burden samples, with orthogonal DNA measurements. Freeze control selection and baseline definitions before evaluation. Compare internal/external/self-centered results with donor-held-out reference checks and DNA at prespecified loci; report reference acceptance/abstention, event sensitivity, false calls and uncertainty stratified by coverage. Advance a baseline only if prespecified tolerances for control mismatch, held-out error and reference sensitivity are met; thresholds must be fixed before seeing results. A stable self-baseline alone is not an absolute-CN pass. The shared 300-cell panel is a feasibility subset, not independent validation.
