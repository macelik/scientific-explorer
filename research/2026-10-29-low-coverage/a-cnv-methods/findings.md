# A. Existing CNV methods — bounded first pass

**Decision.** Keep the existing epiAneufinder family as the direct scATAC relative-depth baseline; investigate Alleloscope's regional allelic model and assess the published Numbat-multiome ATAC-bin caller as a direct candidate. None supplies an established, ready-to-use, reference-free absolute copy number or homozygous deletion call from the current summaries. MCF10-A is non-malignant, not presumed uniformly diploid; the goal is a general method, not a cell-line-specific classifier. Agreement between callers is not biological validation.

## Ranked components and constraints

1. **Existing depth segmentation and QC.** The published epiAneufinder workflow filters cells/bins, GC-corrects, segments via Anderson–Darling distance and uses genome-wide mean as baseline. It cannot detect whole-genome shifts in its baseline and does not provide absolute copies. Low coverage increased false loss calls in the downsampling study, whose full-coverage comparison is not independent truth. The Python reimplementation is described as beta and GPL-3.0; pin the actual repository version before any comparison. [Paper, algorithm and downsampling](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10511508/); [repository](https://github.com/colomemaria/pyEpiAneufinder).
2. **Alleloscope's region-aware allelic estimation.** Its EM estimates SNP phase and per-cell major-haplotype fraction by pooling; relative coverage provides additional information, and the paper reports posterior assignment confidence. The scATAC recommendation is matched DNA segmentation, and the evaluated SNU601 clone assignments used DNA-derived profiles; neither is established here. Segment-wide sums cannot safely reconstruct SNP-level phase or opposing states. Confidence under its model is not automatically calibrated in this dataset. No 0:0 conclusion can be drawn from no allelic observations. [Paper, Methods and scATAC analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9127226/). Official license and code version remain unresolved and must be checked before reuse.
3. **Numbat-multiome ATAC-bin.** This is a published direct scATAC method, correcting the initial RNA-only assessment. Its joint HMM uses Poisson-lognormal bin counts and beta-binomial phased alleles across 15 joint states; it uses iterative clone-aware pooling. The 2025 study benchmarked ATAC-bin against matched WGS in multiple myeloma and CLL/RS cohorts, with ~200 kb average bins and a 50–500 kb resolution study; transfer to a homogeneous no-normal sample is not established. The original RNA model includes (0,0), but this is not evidence of calibrated 0:0 detection in this scATAC dataset. [Paper, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12531993/).

The [official v1.5.2 ATAC-bin vignette](https://kharchenkolab.github.io/numbat/articles/numbat-multiome.html) specifies raw integer bin-by-cell fragment counts, normalized ATAC reference, genomic bins and phased SNP-level allele counts; `get_binned_atac.R`, `pileup_and_phase.R` and `run_numbat_multiome.R` prepare and run it. The published references used normal cells; no suitable normal reference has been identified here. Existing raw bin counts require counting-unit and bin compatibility checks, and bin-level HA/HB sums are not the documented SNP-level input. The vignette describes `--mode ATAC-bin` but its displayed command omits it: verify the source CLI before execution. Joint same-nucleus 10x multiome is described as not fully supported, distinct from separately profiled same-sample assays. No local run was performed.

**Licenses and versions.** Official [pyEpi metadata](https://github.com/colomemaria/pyEpiAneufinder/blob/main/pyproject.toml) declares version 0.3.7 and GPL-3.0-only. [Numbat DESCRIPTION](https://github.com/kharchenkolab/numbat/blob/main/DESCRIPTION) declares 1.5.2 and MIT + [LICENSE](https://github.com/kharchenkolab/numbat/blob/main/LICENSE) (copyright metadata). Exact commits are not pinned. Alleloscope's official DESCRIPTION could not be retrieved; a [third-party package mirror](https://rdrr.io/github/seasoncloud/Alleloscope/) reports version 1.0.1 and GPL-2, but current official terms remain unverified; do not assume permission based on that mirror.

Read depth and allelic total can arise from coupled observations; do not count them as independent without a justified joint model. No allelic observations, observed balance, and adequate evidence against a specified imbalance are distinct. WNN groups are not established clones or normal populations. Sparse SNPs, mapping bias, phase switching, mixture and SNP ascertainment require explicit assessment; neither low AT nor excess zeros identifies deletion. Low-resolution smoothing cannot add independent evidence where observations are absent.

## Next experiment and limits

After the coordinator freezes shared inputs, perform the input-only compatibility audit in [pilot.md](pilot.md): reconcile barcodes and intervals and determine whether phased SNP-level counts and phase-set spans actually exist for each candidate region. Pass only when those inputs and coverage are demonstrably available; otherwise retain depth-only and explicitly unresolved allelic status. This does not establish power: a follow-up must quantify uncertainty for specified alternatives and benchmark on independently characterized DNA datasets, including no-normal settings.

This is a bounded screen, not an exhaustive review.
