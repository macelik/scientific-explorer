# Representation and foundation models: decision

**Decision:** no model is ready for a CN inference experiment on the current inputs. Atacformer earns a **conditional later representation diagnostic**, not a CN caller: proceed only after authentic per-cell peaks or fragments, reference assembly, compatible vocabulary and license are verified, and a held-out dataset with independent DNA CN measurements is available. PEAKVI and SCALE are lower-priority trained baselines. None of the reviewed outputs establishes a calibrated CN state, absolute ploidy, or homozygous deletion. MCF10-A is a development sample, not regional truth; no labelled normal is assumed.

## Ranked feasibility

| Rank | Method | Input, output, availability and cost | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Atacformer** | Human hg38 per-cell accessible interval sets or fragments mapped by overlap to 890,704-region vocabulary; region and pooled cell embeddings. Pretrained on 1.2 million cells, 30 tissues. [Paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12637716/), [quickstart](https://docs.bedbase.org/geniml/atacformer/quickstart/). Base weights exist on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/databio/atacformer-base-hg38), approximately 0.2B parameters; quickstart optionally uses GPU. geniml is [BSD-2-Clause](https://github.com/databio/geniml); weight license not shown on inspected card. | Conditional zero-shot diagnostic. Current 100 kb depth bins cannot faithfully reconstruct open regions; actual peak/fragment input and confirmed build are prerequisites. |
| 2 | **PEAKVI** | Per-cell peak accessibility counts, generally binarized for the detection model; dataset-fit latent and region accessibility probabilities. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2022.100182), [API](https://docs.scvi-tools.org/en/latest/api/reference/scvi.model.PEAKVI.html), [BSD-3-Clause](https://github.com/scverse/scvi-tools/blob/main/LICENSE). No general pretrained checkpoint assumed; training and resources depend on data. | Optional accessibility baseline after peak inputs exist; no CN probability or allele model. |
| 3 | **SCALE** | Peak-by-cell counts, VAE/Gaussian mixture, dataset-trained embeddings, clusters and imputation. [Published paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6783552/), [MIT code and options](https://github.com/jsxlei/SCALE). GPU recommended, default 30,000 maximum iterations; saved model is a dataset checkpoint, not evidence of universal pretrained weights. | Secondary comparator only; training and feature filtering make it less immediately feasible. |
| 4 | **ChromFound** | Human hg38 H5AD peak coordinates, 0-based half-open, plus chromosome vocabulary; embedding and annotation. Pretrained on 1.97M cells; external checkpoints required. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12638), [official v1.0.0 repository](https://github.com/SAIS-LifeScience/ChromFound): Apache-2.0 code, CUDA 12.1+, FlashAttention-capable GPU and Mamba. Weight terms must be checked separately. | Defer: costly environment and unavailable inputs, with no demonstrated CN benefit. |
| 5 | **EpiFoundation** | Nonzero peak sets and chromosome/peak vocabulary; pretrained by peak-to-gene alignment on >100,000 paired human GRCh38 multiome cells from 19 tissues. Embedding, cell type or gene expression; [preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11839112/), [official repository and external weights](https://github.com/UCSC-VLAA/EpiFoundation). Code and weights licensing not established; FlashAttention2 architecture; inference resource needs unknown. | Defer: peak vocabulary/compatibility and license unresolved, and RNA prediction is indirect for CN. |
| 6 | **scBasset** | Matched peak-by-cell matrix, peak sequences and genome FASTA; sequence-based accessibility prediction and cell representations. [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-022-01562-8), [Apache-2.0 code and preprocessing](https://github.com/calico/scBasset). Default training up to 1000 epochs with early stopping; existing weights are dataset-specific and not established as a transferable CN predictor. | Defer: input and training costs with a binary accessibility objective that does not identify dosage. |

Ranks indicate practical research priority, not measured predictive performance. The evidence is scATAC representation evaluation; transfer to CN detection and uncertainty is untested. Source and record details, including unverified fields, are in `sources.json` and `methods.json`. Atacformer and EpiFoundation papers were reviewed as preprints; ChromFound has a NeurIPS 2025 poster and preprint. Publication and repository maintenance status can change; versions should be pinned before any experiment.

## Why a representation is not independent CN evidence

CN differences can affect accessibility counts and therefore the embedding; cell state, sequencing depth, batch and technical dropout can also do so. Selecting groups or a reference using an embedding driven by a putative CN event and then treating that same event's separation as confirmation is circular. Conversely, binary peak presence, per-cell normalization, token pooling, feature selection or batch correction may reduce dosage signal. Their effect here is **an inference and a testable risk**, not an established result for these data. A cell-type or tumour label, an accessibility probability, and a well-separated cluster are not calibrated CN calls. Imputation does not create independent evidence in missing regions. These models provide no established out-of-distribution CN calibration in the sources inspected.

## Next experiment and decision criterion

First establish authentic local peak/fragments, same cell barcode identity, exact reference build and 0-based coordinate conversion, checkpoint/vocabulary coverage, weight terms, and memory budget. If any gate fails, stop. Then on independent held-out samples with orthogonal DNA truth, compare zero-shot Atacformer embeddings against a simple peak-based baseline, both with and without masking held-out CN regions from representation construction. Quantify association of embeddings with depth and sample, and evaluate prespecified independent event separation and reproducibility across coverage strata and datasets. Adopt only as an exploratory covariate if it improves held-out event discrimination without increased confounding or leakage; thresholds and uncertainty intervals must be specified before observing outcomes. No normal reference or diploid anchor is presumed. This is not a substitute for measuring CN event/boundary accuracy and calibrated uncertainty in a CN caller.

No pilot was run; see `pilot.md` for the compatibility gate and rationale. The general scATAC CNV objective remains broader than this sample and these representation tasks.
