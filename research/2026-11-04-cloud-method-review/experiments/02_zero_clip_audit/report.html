CDKN2A/CDKN2B (published MCF10A homozygous deletion)chr921802636220093133120
The bins covering CDKN2A/CDKN2B, reported as homozygously deleted in MCF10-A, are absent from the retained grid. They are not blacklisted (they are in the post-blacklist grid) and contain no N bases, so the ≥15%-nonzero-cells bin filter removed them. The pipeline cannot call this event; it becomes a gap between retained neighbours. Any clonal homozygous deletion (or a region inaccessible in at least 85% of cells) behaves the same way, in any dataset.

Interpretation

  • Removal by the bin filter is outcome-dependent: the stronger and more clonal the loss, the more likely its bins disappear. A deletion-aware method needs the pre-filter grid with an explicit 'removed: too few nonzero cells' state, not silent gaps. Without pre-QC counts we cannot say what the other removed runs contain.
  • Rounding zeros depend on coverage: low-coverage cells lose a larger share of raw-positive bins. That is a coverage-dependent distortion of the processed measurement, avoided by modelling raw counts with exposure.
