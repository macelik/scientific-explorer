Experiment 01 · pinned pyEpi GC correction rebuilt on retained raw counts

Question

Does the pinned pyEpi source (e8305ea, pyEpiAneufinder.py L252–L271 with get_loess_smoothed L155–L164) reproduce the saved X from layers/raw and var.GC? What happens to the floating values before integer casting?

Result

3449 / 3460 cells reproduce bit-for-bit. 11 cells differ, in 17 bins in total; in every differing bin our rebuild's LOESS prediction is near zero (range [0.0001923150266520679, 0.013288842514157295]). Recovering the historical integers from saved X (residual ≤ 5.7e-14) shows the historical value was {'historical_zero': 17} at those bins. This is consistent with platform/library-dependent sign or magnitude of a near-zero LOESS denominator, not with a different formula. The historical run environment is not recorded, so this remains a provenance finding.

No fit fell back to the raw vector; no non-finite float occurred in this environment. 2969 cell-bins in 327 cells have LOESS prediction ≤ 0 (GC range of affected bins 0.224–0.322; 100% below the 1st GC percentile): the LOESS boundary.

Raw-positive values that become zero

544,584 raw-positive cell-bins are 0 in saved X (0.88% of raw-positive cell-bins). Rebuild attribution: 543,217 by np.rint of a value in [0, 0.5) and 1,350 by negative-to-zero clipping (negative LOESS prediction). No raw-zero cell-bin becomes positive.

All tested environments (Python 3.10/3.13, numpy 1.24/2.5, scikit-misc 0.1.4–0.5.3) give the same predictions to ≤1e-13 and none gives a non-positive prediction at the 17 bins that are zero in the saved X. The historical cause of those zeros is therefore unresolved (a different execution path, guard, version or platform is possible; the run environment was not recorded). It affects 17 of 87.7 million cell-bins.

Interpretation

  • The saved X is the pinned formula applied to the saved raw layer and GC (bit-identical for 99.7% of cells); the remaining differences sit exactly where correction = mean/fit has a near-zero denominator.
  • The formula has no guard for small or negative predictions: a negative prediction zeroes a raw-positive bin, a tiny positive one multiplies it by hundreds to thousands. Both are measurement artefacts of the GC step, observed here at the GC boundary.
  • Rounding to integers removes about 0.9% of raw-positive cell-bins, concentrated in cells and bins with low expected counts; see experiment 02.
  • This check does not show whether these artefacts create any called segment; that is tested in experiments 04–05.
