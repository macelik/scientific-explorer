Bottom line

The beta-binomial haplotype-fraction model recovers the strong controls (MYC h = 0.593, 5q13-qter h = 0.605) and finds no allelic imbalance at the six saved chr7/chr9 windows, but its nominal chi-square calibration fails in uncalled random windows (24% exceed 3.84), so it is consistent with serving only as an empirical-null-calibrated diagnostic, not yet as an evidence layer.

Key findings

  1. MYC control: phase-agnostic h = 0.593 (95% interval 0.574-0.612, LR = 73); 5q13-qter h = 0.605 (0.601-0.607); 19q13-qter h = 0.527 (0.520-0.534). The 9p21 zone is not distinguishable from balance (h = 0.525, 0.500-0.570).
  2. Saved windows (pooled, rho empirical): chr7_8.3_10.7 h = 0.537 (0.500-0.573); chr7_51.4_53.5 h = 0.500 (0.500-0.565); chr7_67.4_70.0 h = 0.500 (0.500-0.565); chr7_70.0_73.3 h = 0.545 (0.500-0.572); chr9_20_27 h = 0.510 (0.500-0.547); chr9_22.5_26.6 h = 0.517 (0.500-0.575). chr9_70_100 (comparison window, 282 bins) h = 0.527 with LR = 27, small but significant given AT = 131,184.
  3. Calibration is poor at the empirical rho 0.0038: 24.3% of 226 uncalled random windows exceed LR 3.84 (agnostic; oriented 12.8%), versus 5% nominal; the empirical 95th percentile is LR = 16.4. With rho = 0.02 the exceedance falls to 0.0% and with rho = 0 it rises to 85.4%.
  4. Power at h = 0.75 (size-calibrated 0.05, ICC 0.02, agnostic): 1.00 in all 7 saved windows (1,000 reps, MC SE <= 0.01). At h = 0.6 power depends strongly on ICC: median 1.00 (ICC 0), 0.79 (ICC 0.02), 0.21 (ICC 0.10); range 0.14-0.97 at ICC 0.10.
  5. Phase switches: the agnostic model's power is the same across orientation scenarios by construction, but the oriented model at h = 0.75 falls from 1.00 (none) to 0.83 (one switch) and 0.58 (random orientation), median across saved windows.
  6. Attenuated scenario (MYC effective |dBAF| = 0.106): agnostic power 0.86-1.00 across saved windows; at half that imbalance 0.20-1.00, so weaker real-world imbalance would often be missed in the shortest windows.
  7. Labels (pooled, rho empirical): saved windows 6 'against h = 0.75' and 1 'consistent with imbalance' (chr9_70_100); literature 3 consistent / 1 against (9p21); 300 random windows: 43 'consistent with imbalance' (14%) even after using the empirical threshold, and no region is labelled 'insufficient' because the h = 0.75 alternative is large.
  8. Oriented vs agnostic: for MYC the oriented estimate is h = 0.448 (below 0.5) against agnostic 0.593, and 5q13-qter oriented 0.507 versus agnostic 0.605; the large drop in oriented LR (e.g. 5q13-qter 22 vs 4395) is consistent with GT-based orientation not being consistent between bins.

Question and why it matters

Method record E:2 ‘Phase-aware beta-binomial allele model’ (research/2026-10-29-low-coverage/synthesis/methods_inventory.json; e-per-cell-evidence/findings.md). It asks whether modelling the allele counts HB | AT as beta-binomial with a haplotype fraction h, with and without trusting the GT-based phase orientation, gives (i) interpretable estimates with intervals, (ii) a null that is calibrated on regions that were not called, and (iii) enough power at the prespecified alternatives (NEXT_EXPERIMENTS.md section 2, Allelic arm). The decision it informs is whether E:2 can enter an allelic evidence layer next to depth-based calls, and with which evidence labels. Exp 06 used a phase-agnostic bin-level statistic S and found strong attenuation relative to nominal counts; this experiment replaces S by a likelihood with an explicit orientation model.

Inputs

  • HA / HB / AT parquet (aligned_matrices; 25,337 bins x 3,460 barcodes, float32 integer-valued), joined to AnnData by barcode name; coordinates and barcode sets checked equal; AT = HA + HB verified for the pooled and all five cluster sums.
  • Groups: pooled (3,460 cells) and WNN clusters (wnn_leiden_0.3) 0-4 (1,286 / 833 / 603 / 556 / 182 cells).
  • Regions (exp 04 families): 7 saved windows (6 chr7/chr9 windows plus chr9_70_100 as comparison), 67 called short segments, 4 literature loci, 300 random 25-bin windows (226 uncalled in the all karyogram).
  • karyogram_cluster_segments.tsv (group all) for the called/uncalled status and for base-called bins used for rho; exp 06 summary.json for the MYC effective |dBAF|.
  • Units: counts per 100 kb bin. Not available: unique fragments or molecules (AT sums per-SNP-site depth), phase-set IDs, a verified cross-bin phase orientation, a matched normal, copy-number truth.

Method

Step 1. Per region and group take bins with AT ≥ 1. Step 2. Fit two models by grid maximum likelihood (delta step 0.0025, |delta| ≤ 0.49).

HB_j | AT_j ~ BetaBinomial(AT_j, p_j, rho),  a = p(1-rho)/rho,  b = (1-p)(1-rho)/rho  (rho = 0: Binomial)
phase-agnostic: p_j = 0.5 + delta or 0.5 - delta, prob 1/2 each, independent per bin; delta in [0, 0.49]
     log L(delta) = sum_j log[ 0.5 f(HB_j; p=0.5+delta) + 0.5 f(HB_j; p=0.5-delta) ]
oriented:       p_j = 0.5 + delta for all j; delta in [-0.49, 0.49]
h = 0.5 + delta;  LR = 2 (max log L - log L(0));  95% interval = {delta: 2 (max log L - log L(delta)) <= 3.841}
evidence against h = 0.75: LR75 = 2 (max log L - log L(0.25)) > 3.841

Step 3. rho is fixed at the empirical value 0.00384 (median over five AT strata of bins in base-called all segments, cn_call = 1, AT ≥ 20; identical to exp 06) and, as sensitivity, at 0, 0.02, 0.10.

Step 4 (calibration). The same fit is applied to the 300 random windows pooled; uncalled windows (no bin with cn_call ≠ 1) give an empirical null for the LR; its 95th percentile (agnostic 16.36, oriented 6.59) is the threshold for the ‘consistent with imbalance’ label.

Step 5 (prospective power). Generator numpy PCG64 default_rng((20261029, stream, task, scenario)), stream 1 = power grid, 2 = label simulations; 1,000 replicates per scenario. Data: each saved window's own pooled AT profile (and cluster 0's); h in {0.5, 0.6, 0.75, 0.9} x data ICC {0, 0.02, 0.10} x phase {none, random per bin, one switch at a random interior bin}; plus the attenuated scenario with delta = MYC effective |dBAF| and half of it (ICC = empirical). Analysis rho either the fixed empirical value (‘empirical’) or the generating ICC (‘matched’). Size is calibrated per cell by the 95th percentile of the h = 0.5 LR (shared across phase scenarios since the sign is irrelevant at h = 0.5); power = fraction of alternative replicates above it, with binomial MC SE. Simulation grid step 0.01.

Step 6 (labels, pooled, agnostic, rho empirical): allele unobserved (< 3 bins with AT ≥ 1); evidence consistent with imbalance (interval excludes 0.5 and LR above the empirical random-window q95); insufficient information (simulated power < 0.8 for h = 0.75 with random orientation); evidence against specified imbalance (LR75 > 3.84 and power ≥ 0.8); otherwise compatible with balance. The ‘against’ label is conditional on treating AT as independent observations, which exp 06 showed is optimistic.

Pooled estimates for the empirical rho and the prespecified rho values. Larger assumed rho widens intervals and pulls weak estimates to 0.5, so weak apparent imbalance (chr7_8.3_10.7, chr7_70.0_73.3, chr9_70_100) is sensitive to rho, whereas MYC and 5q13-qter are not.

Across the prespecified alternatives: h = 0.75 and 0.9 are detected with power ≥ 0.83 in all windows at all ICCs (lowest: chr9_22.5_26.6, ICC 0.10, h = 0.75); h = 0.6 is detected reliably only at low ICC and in deep windows.

Limits and alternative explanations

  • AT is summed site depth: one fragment can contribute at several SNP sites, so counts are not independent. This inflates the effective n and is why the ‘against’ label (and every power value) is conditional on that assumption; exp 06 showed the assumption is optimistic.
  • Calibration failure: with the empirical rho the null is anti-conservative in real uncalled windows; the empirical q95 is therefore used for ‘consistent with imbalance’. Even so, 43 of 300 random windows receive that label, which indicates widespread allelic structure or model misfit outside depth calls.
  • A significant LR is also consistent with technical causes (genotype or phasing errors, reference bias, ascertainment, mixed cell states), not only a copy-number change.
  • Phase orientation across bins is GT-based (Eagle) and unverified; agreement between oriented and agnostic fits would not verify phase, and there are no phase-set IDs.
  • Fixed rho does not capture region-specific overdispersion; grid resolution 0.01 in simulations; labels are for pooled all-cell data and dilute subclonal events.
  • The phase-agnostic power is independent of the orientation scenario by construction. MCF10-A is development data, not CN truth; the power values describe the stated model scenarios, not a validated operating threshold.

What the evidence supports

Supports

  • Strong allelic signal at MYC and 5q13-qter is recovered with intervals well away from 0.5 (consistent with a copy-number gain in cells in which sites remain observable).
  • No imbalance of the h = 0.75 type in the six saved chr7/chr9 windows; these windows have power ~1 for that alternative under the model.
  • Oriented and phase-agnostic fits diverge in the gain controls, consistent with orientation inconsistency between bins.

Does not support / cannot tell

  • Calibrated nominal p-values: uncalled random windows exceed the nominal cutoff far more often than 5% at the empirical rho.
  • Any claim that the saved windows are balanced at weaker imbalance (h around 0.55-0.6): power there is limited and depends on ICC.
  • Verified phase orientation, or an absolute copy-number state, from these counts.
  • The ‘against’ label as a reliable exclusion: it relies on AT counting independent observations.

Decision impact

E:2 can enter an evidence layer now only as an exploratory, empirically thresholded diagnostic (phase-agnostic LR compared with an uncalled-window null; oriented fit reported as a phase-consistency check). The nominal-chi-square version should not be used, the ‘against specified imbalance’ label should stay flagged as conditional, and labels need a calibrated rho (a value between the empirical 0.0038 and 0.02 would be needed for near-nominal size; not tested here) or a unique-molecule count before any threshold is treated as operating guidance. This adds a likelihood-based allelic arm to the recommendation in research/2026-11-04-cloud-method-review/recommendation.md but does not change the depth-based recommendation.

Next checks

  • Re-run calibration with rho chosen so that uncalled random windows give about 5% exceedance, and report the resulting power loss (rho between 0.0038 and 0.02).
  • Obtain unique-fragment or molecule counts (or phase blocks) and repeat the fit with them in place of AT.
  • Add a region-level random-effect or bin-block bootstrap to replace fixed rho.
  • Fit chr9_70_100 and the 14 called short segments labelled 'consistent' on cluster subsets to see whether imbalance is clonal or cluster-specific.
  • Check orientation consistency with an external phased reference (phase sets) before trusting the oriented model.
