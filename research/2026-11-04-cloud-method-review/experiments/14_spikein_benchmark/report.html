Bottom line

On this cluster-0 background the baseline pipeline recovered injected events only when they were at least 20 bins wide and carried by most cells (pooled detection at widths 20-50: 0.09; widths 5-10: 0.00); this is a semi-synthetic detectability measurement, not biological sensitivity.

Key findings

  1. Width 50, all cells (f=1.0): detection loss_one 0.17 (95% CI 0.03-0.56, n=6); loss_deep 0.80 (95% CI 0.38-0.96, n=5); gain 0.00 (95% CI 0.00-0.43, n=5).
  2. Width 50, 25% of cells (f=0.25): loss_one 0.00 (95% CI 0.00-0.39, n=6); loss_deep 0.00 (95% CI 0.00-0.43, n=5); gain 0.00 (95% CI 0.00-0.43, n=5).
  3. Widths 5 and 10 bins: pooled detection 0.00 (0/102); the workbench min-child rule (20 bins) forbids segments this short, so this is structural.
  4. Boundary accuracy at widths >=20 (nearest boundary within 10 bins of an edge, n=63 edges of 292 with any boundary): median |offset| 1.0 bins, fraction within +-2 bins 0.15.
  5. False new boundaries on the rescanned chromosome: mean 0.86 per realization; 0.35 of realizations had at least one; mean reference boundaries lost 0.67.
  6. Harness check: 22/22 unmodified chromosome scans reproduced the saved cluster0 breakpoints; CN calls identical to saved karyogram: True; X reconstruction exact for 1284/1286 cells.
  7. Measured injected contrast (raw target/flank, after/before) at f=1.0 ranged 0.09-1.51; at f=0.25 0.75-1.13, inside the natural 0.44-1.72x range of uncalled windows (experiment 04) for most loss realizations.
  8. Ran 248 realizations (5-6 loci of the 40 prespecified per scenario, 45 scenarios), so intervals are wide.

Question and why it matters

NEXT_EXPERIMENTS.md section 3 needs event-size-stratified evaluation: the app's width series describes specific realizations, not detection probabilities. This benchmark injects known events into real cluster-0 raw counts and asks how the current baseline pipeline (AD recursive segmentation, k=6, 1000 permutations, alpha 0.001, min child 20 / terminal 50 bins; pinned assign_gainloss_new) responds, as a function of width, depth, carrier-cell fraction and local baseline.

Semi-synthetic simulation on a real background; not biological ground truth and not a calibrated sensitivity.

Inputs

  • pyepi_results/count_matrix.h5ad: layers/raw, X, var.GC (read-only).
  • WNN cluster 0 labels via common.cell_clusters (integrated.h5mu): 1286 cells.
  • integration-story/data/genome_pool/raw_accepted_breakpoints.tsv (saved cluster0 AD breakpoints, chromosome-local) and derived/karyogram_cluster_segments.tsv (harness).
  • Pinned pyEpi e8305ea: render_fragments.get_loess_smoothed, assign_gainloss_new, AD/permutation tests; workbench server/allelic_scan.py, server/cn_recall.py.
  • Loci rule: autosomes, gap-free event (all 100 kb start steps), ≥60 bins from chromosome ends, ≥10 bins from any saved cluster0 breakpoint.
  • Not available: independent copy-number truth, allelic information in this simulation, any real event with known cell fraction; only the raw-count model is perturbed.

Design

Chromosome-local event bins [s, s+w); chosen cells = random fraction f of the cluster (seed tuple (20261104, 14, scenario, locus)). R = raw counts of a chosen cell in the event bins; flank = 50 bins each side.

loss_one :  R' ~ Binomial(R, 0.5)
loss_deep:  R' ~ Binomial(R, 0.1)
gain     :  R' = R + Poisson(0.5 * shape_b * flank_rate_cell)
            flank_rate_cell = mean raw count of the cell over the flank bins
            shape_b = pb_raw[b] / mean(pb_raw[flank])   (cluster-0 raw pseudobulk)
X' (modified cells) = pinned L252-L271: loess(GC, counts) -> counts*mean/fit -> rint -> clip<0 -> /(sum/1e5)
pb' = pb_savedX + sum_cells(X' - X_recon_unmodified)/n
detected = boundary within +-2 bins of BOTH edges AND CN call (loss <1, gain >1) on >=50% of event bins

Grid: 3 types x widths {5,10,20,30,50} x f {1.0,0.5,0.25} = 45 scenarios. 40 loci per scenario are prespecified; 5-6 were run per scenario (loci taken in order, locus-major) because each realization needs LOESS for up to 1,286 cells plus a chromosome AD scan (~10-25 s), and the budget was 45 minutes on 2 workers. Nothing else in the grid was dropped. Only the affected chromosome is rescanned; CN is re-called genome-wide using saved boundaries elsewhere.

Seeds: loci (20261104, 14, scenario); cells and noise (20261104, 14, scenario, locus), numpy PCG64. The pinned permutation tests use their own fixed random_state=42, so scans are deterministic given input.

Limits and alternative explanations

  • Semi-synthetic: thinning and Poisson addition model a dosage change as a proportional change in observed counts; real events also change accessibility, GC and mappability structure, which is preserved here by construction. This measures detectability on this background only.
  • Conditional on random loci, cluster 0 (1,286 cells) and this subset of loci; not a calibrated sensitivity or false-positive rate.
  • Loci avoid chromosome ends by 60 bins and existing breakpoints by 10 bins, which favours detection compared with hard cases.
  • Detection at widths below 20 is structurally impossible under the min-child rule, an alternative explanation for their zero rate.
  • Baseline CN calls already deviate from 1 in some regions; detected_net separates this. Only the affected chromosome is rescanned.
  • The gain model's Poisson noise is not overdispersed like real cells.

What the evidence supports

Supports

  • Whether this pseudobulk-AD pipeline can place both edges of injected events as a function of width and carrier fraction, on this background.
  • That boundary offsets and extra boundaries can be quantified per scenario against a known answer.

Does not support / cannot tell

  • Biological sensitivity or specificity, or the true fraction of cells in a real subclone.
  • Performance for events whose accessibility also changes, or on other clusters or samples.
  • That a failed detection means the event is absent in real data.

Decision impact

For research/2026-11-04-cloud-method-review/recommendation.md: the baseline can be described as able to recover events of at least 20 bins when most cells carry them and the pseudobulk contrast leaves the natural range, and as unable by construction to return segments shorter than 20 bins. Detection at low carrier fractions is given by the tables above (conditional on this simulation); statements about small or subclonal events in the app should cite these rates as exploratory, not as calibrated detection probabilities.

Next checks

  • Run the remaining prespecified loci (up to 40 per scenario) to narrow the intervals.
  • Repeat on another cluster and on the all-cell pseudobulk, and with a lower min-child setting as an exploratory alternative.
  • Add an accessibility-altering injection arm and per-cell overdispersed gain noise.
  • Rescan the whole genome for false boundaries elsewhere, not only the affected chromosome.
