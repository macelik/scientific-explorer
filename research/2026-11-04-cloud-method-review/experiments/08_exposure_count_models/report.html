Interpretation

  • With thousands of cells, Poisson, quasi-Poisson and cluster-robust intervals for the rate ratio are narrow. Almost every region, random ones included, is 'significant' against rate ratio 1. Correcting overdispersion (quasi/NB) fixes sampling variance; it does not supply a null for regional baseline structure.
  • Against the empirical spread of uncalled random windows (MAD of log rr = 0.391), 27 of 46 called short losses and 4 of 21 called short gains remain unusual at BH q < 0.05. The random windows are not neutral truth, so these are relative-unusualness statements.
  • The heterogeneity tests show whether a contrast varies with coverage (a technical signature) or across clusters (a candidate subclonal or cell-state difference). The held-out zero check shows whether NB already explains the zeros, which is the precondition E:3 sets before any zero-inflation model.

Poisson badly underpredicts the zero-count cells in the deep chr7 windows; NB with a single moment-estimated alpha predicts them within about 2 SD on held-out cells. The E:3 precondition (NB misfit) is therefore not met there, and a zero-inflated component is not needed to describe these zeros.

Limits

  • Accessibility, mappability and CN are confounded in every column; cluster adjustment can absorb real signal.
  • Called segments were selected on these same data; their empirical p-values are conditional on selection.
  • Exposure excludes the focal region and flanks but still contains other events.
