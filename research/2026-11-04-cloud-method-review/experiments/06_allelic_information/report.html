Bottom line

The chr7 saved windows keep allele observations (16–23 bins with AT ≥ 5) and show no excess allelic variance (effective |dBAF| upper bound ≤ 0.05), which would have been detectable if as strong as at MYC (power 0.85–1.00) but usually not at half that strength; nominal AT overstates allelic information (MYC effective |dBAF| 0.11 against a nominal prediction of S ≈ 145 versus observed 43.8).

Key findings

  1. Alignment: AT = HA + HB exactly for every cell and bin (True); values are non-negative integers (True); coordinates match the AnnData; barcode sets are equal but column order differs, so the join is by name.
  2. Overdispersion: ρ estimated on base-called bins is 0.0038 (median over 5 AT strata, range 0.0036–0.0046), while the variance inflation rises from 1.20 to 6.04 with AT.
  3. The balanced beta-binomial model underpredicts allelic variance in uncalled random windows: 71 of 226 (31%) exceed their own model 95th percentile (38% of all 300 random windows); the empirical 95th percentile of S in uncalled random windows is 13.4.
  4. Attenuation at positive controls: MYC S = 43.8 (model 95th percentile 10.7), effective |dBAF| 0.106 (bootstrap 95% 0.000–0.164), far below the nominal clonal-gain value 0.167 that would give S ≈ 145; 5q13-qter 0.109; 19q13-qter 0.049.
  5. chr7 saved windows: pooled AT 573–3,359; S = 0.60–2.48 against model 95th percentiles 1.66–2.47; effective |dBAF| = 0.00 in all four; selected-site observations per fragment 0.44–0.76x the flanks.
  6. Power anchored to MYC’s effective imbalance (0.106) is 0.85–1.00 for the chr7 windows; at half that (0.053) it is 0.23–0.77. Theoretical powers for nominal one-copy changes are 1.0 and are upper bounds not used for labels.
  7. 9p21 hemizygous zone: 0.20x genome selected-site observations per fragment (20 of 38 bins have AT = 0), the lowest of the literature loci, as expected when clonal LOH removes heterozygous sites.
  8. Labels over 378 regions: random windows 38 of 300 'consistent with imbalance'; short called losses 5 of 46; saved windows 1 of 7 (chr9_70_100 comparison). The MYC control is labelled ‘no excess imbalance at observed sites’ although its S exceeds the model 95th percentile, because the label rule also requires a bootstrap lower bound of effective |dBAF| above 0 and 15 informative bins give a lower bound of 0.000.

Question and why it matters

Question. For each fixed region, are allele observations present, how many selected-site observations come per fragment, could a specified imbalance be detected, and which evidence label is supported? Missing alleles are kept distinct from balance.

Why it matters. The recommendation proposes an allelic observability and calibrated-label component in the per-region evidence layer, and a joint depth–allele model later. Both rest on whether allelic data are informative at this coverage and whether nominal counts can be used as independent observations. The chr7 dips are the key case: a clonal one-copy loss would normally leave allelic imbalance at heterozygous sites, while a shared baseline effect would not.

Inputs

  • Allelic matrices. aligned_matrices/HA.parquet, HB.parquet, AT.parquet: bins × cells (25,337 × 3,460); HA and HB are the two haplotype counts and AT their sum, taken from selected phased heterozygous SNP-site depth (DP) summed per bin. AT is a site-observation count, not established unique fragments. Pooled over all cells.
  • Depth. layers/raw of count_matrix.h5ad (fragments), WNN labels not used here.
  • Regions. The same rule-defined families as experiment 04 (imported from its code): 7 saved (6 windows + chr9 70–100 Mb comparison), 67 short called segments of the all source, 4 literature loci (9p21 zone approx., MYC 8q24, 19q13-qter, 5q13-qter), 300 random 25-bin windows.
  • Base-called bins (cn_call = 1 in the all karyogram, AT ≥ 20) are used only to estimate ρ; calls are not truth.
  • Not available. SNP-level counts, phase-set IDs, unique-molecule counts and a matched normal; clonal LOH regions therefore lose ascertainment and imbalance there cannot be measured at sites that were never selected.

Method

  1. Check alignment (coordinates, barcode set, AT = HA + HB, integer values) and join by barcode name.
  2. Estimate beta-binomial overdispersion ρ from the variance of z2 in base-called bins stratified by AT quintile; use the median over strata.
  3. For each region compute S, the mean z2 over bins with AT ≥ 5; phase-agnostic, so inconsistent orientation between bins does not cancel signal.
  4. Model null: simulate beta-binomial (p = 0.5, ρ) with each region’s own AT per bin; threshold = 95th percentile of S. Empirical null: 95th percentile of S over uncalled random windows.
  5. Power: same simulation with p = 0.5 ± δ (random sign per bin). Scenarios: nominal one-copy loss in a fraction f of cells, nominal one-copy gain, and imbalance anchored to the effective |dBAF| measured at the published MYC amplification and half of it.
  6. Effective |dBAF| (δeff) from the bias-corrected mean of z2; bootstrap 95% interval over bins (500 resamples).
  7. Label from preset rules (below).
z_j^2      = (HB_j - HA_j)^2 / AT_j           # per bin, AT_j >= 5
S          = mean_j z_j^2
Var(z^2)   under BB:  v_j = 1 + (AT_j - 1) * rho
delta_eff  = sqrt(max(0, mean_j (z_j^2 - v_j) / (4 AT_j)))
rho_stratum = (median(z^2)/0.4549 - 1) / (median_AT - 1)    # 0.4549 = median of chi2_1
loss (fraction f):  delta = f / (2 (2 - f));  gain:  delta = f / (2 (2 + f))
label:  unobserved if bins_AT>=5 < 3;  consistent with imbalance if S > S_null_model_q95 and CI_lo(delta_eff) > 0;
        insufficient information if power(MYC-anchored) < 0.8;  else no excess imbalance at observed sites

Seeds: numpy.random.default_rng((20261104, 6, region_index)) for the bootstrap and the model null; (20261104, 6, region_index, 1) for power simulations; 2,000 simulations per scenario. There is no ‘against loss’ label: the attenuation of the alternative is not calibrated (see below).

Sensitivity

The alternatives are anchored to MYC’s effective imbalance and half of it (0.106 and 0.053); nominal scenarios give an upper bound. ρ was estimated per AT stratum (0.0036–0.0046) and the median used. Not varied: the AT ≥ 5 cut, the 2,000 simulations, the 500-resample bootstrap and the 0.8 power cut in the label rule.

Limits and alternative explanations

  • AT sums SNP-site DP; one fragment can count at several sites, which ρ partly absorbs. Unique-molecule counting and SNP-level phase blocks are not available in this package.
  • Power assumes the region’s sites remain ascertained. In clonal LOH without a matched normal, heterozygous sites tend not to be called, so low AT per fragment is the expected signature and imbalance cannot be measured at sites that were never selected.
  • Labels describe pooled all-cell evidence; a subclonal event in one cluster is diluted.
  • The label rule depends on a bootstrap lower bound over few bins; MYC (15 informative bins) is labelled ‘no excess imbalance’ despite S well above the model threshold, so labels near the bin-count limit are fragile.
  • ‘No excess imbalance’ is not ‘balanced’: absence of detectable imbalance with power only for MYC-sized effects.
  • 71 of 226 uncalled random windows exceed the balanced model, so the null is not calibrated for per-window decisions.

What the evidence supports

Supports

  • Allele observations exist in the chr7 saved windows and show no excess allelic variance, with enough power to see a MYC-sized imbalance but usually not half of it.
  • The 9p21 zone has the lowest selected-site observations per fragment, as expected when clonal LOH removes heterozygous sites.
  • MYC and 5q13-qter carry strong allelic excess; nominal counts overstate allelic information, so likelihoods that treat AT as independent observations would be miscalibrated.

Does not support / cannot tell

  • That the chr7 windows are balanced or diploid: only that no excess imbalance is detectable at observed sites, at limited power for weaker effects.
  • Calibrated per-region allelic calls: a large share of uncalled random windows exceed the balanced model.
  • An ‘against clonal loss’ statement: the attenuation of the alternative is not calibrated.

Decision impact

Supports recommendation ranks 1 and 5: carry an empirically calibrated allelic label (unobserved / insufficient / no excess / consistent with imbalance) in the evidence layer, and defer any joint depth–allele likelihood until SNP-level, phase-block-aware, molecule-aware counts exist. The chr7 result does not support a clonal one-copy loss as the sole explanation of the dips but cannot exclude weaker or subclonal changes.

Next checks

  • Obtain SNP-level phased counts with phase-set IDs and unique-molecule counting to calibrate an allelic likelihood.
  • Revisit the label rule for regions with few informative bins (e.g. MYC) once an empirical null for the bootstrap bound exists.
  • Add a matched normal or panel of unrelated samples so LOH-driven ascertainment can be distinguished from technical loss of sites.
