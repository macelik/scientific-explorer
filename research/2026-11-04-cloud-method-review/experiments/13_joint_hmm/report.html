Bottom line

A transparent joint depth + allele HMM reproduces the large chr7 loss-level windows and the MYC gain-level locus as relative states, but only because a large between-bin baseline-variance term is included; the allele layer mainly changes confidence and the imbalance mass, and 9p21 and the weak-gain loci remain insufficient evidence. Exploratory; relative states are not copy number.

Key findings

  1. Baseline variance dominates the depth emission. Cell-sampling dispersion is tiny (phi_s = 1.4e-06 all; the cluster0 half-split estimate was negative and floored at 1e-08) but the between-bin baseline variance in cn_call==1 bins is tau2 = 0.255 (about 0.51 relative SD per bin). Without it the HMM fragments the genome: 17352 depth runs vs 600 in the full model (all-cell pseudobulk), 57% of bins change loss/neutral/gain class, and 4 of 22 region summaries flip class.
  2. chr7 saved windows (all cells): posterior mass on depth-loss states 0.92, 0.97, 1.00, 0.84 (8.3-10.7, 51.4-53.5, 67.4-70.0, 70.0-73.3 Mb); imbalance mass 0.02, 0.04, 0.00, 0.00. The loss-level depth call is consistent with pyEpi cn_call 0 there; the allele layer shows no imbalance excess at the observed sites.
  3. chr9 windows are unresolved: loss mass 9p 20-27 Mb 0.37, 22.5-26.6 Mb 0.70, 9p21 literature zone 0.41, all flagged insufficient evidence (max joint-state probability 0.62/0.38/0.58); only 18 of 38 bins in the 9p21 zone carry any allele observation.
  4. Gain controls: MYC gain mass 1.00 (imbalance mass 1.00, max joint-state 1.00); 5q13-qter gain mass 0.58 (imbalance 0.59, flagged insufficient); 19q13-qter gain mass 0.33 vs loss 0.33 (unresolved). pyEpi cn_call is 2 at MYC and 1 at 5q and 19q.
  5. Agreement with AD and pyEpi (all cells; neither is truth): 79/142 AD breakpoints (56%) have an HMM depth boundary within 2 bins (random-placement reference about 11%), 63% within 5 bins; but only 14% of the 578 HMM depth boundaries lie within 2 bins of an AD breakpoint. Bin-level class agreement with pyEpi cn_call is 63%; the main disagreement is 6377 bins the HMM places at gain level (r > 1) where pyEpi says neutral.
  6. Allele layer in sensitivity: switching it off changes 12% (all) and 5% (cluster0) of Viterbi depth classes, changes the modal depth level in 2 of 22 region summaries, and removes the ability to flag imbalance; it lowers the max joint-state probability (insufficient-evidence flags rise in 8 region summaries, fall in 0).
  7. Calibration warning: 6.7% of pyEpi cn_call==1 bins are Viterbi-imbalanced and 42% have a depth state other than r = 1 (all cells), so state calls in single bins are not a calibrated error rate. 12 of 22 region summaries (full model, both sources) are flagged insufficient evidence.

Question and why it matters

Method record B:3 (covariance-aware joint segmentation / Bayesian HMM, proposed) argues that depth and allele counts should be modelled together, with missing allele data treated as no evidence, distance-aware transitions and abstention. This experiment asks, on the actual MCF10-A development data: (i) what a simple, fully inspectable version of that model says in the six saved windows and four literature loci; (ii) how its relative states and boundaries compare with the AD breakpoints and pyEpi cn_call; and (iii) what the allele layer and the between-bin baseline-variance term each contribute.

Decision informed: whether the review's recommendation should add a joint model (and which component would carry its value) or keep depth-only segmentation with a separate allelic evidence label.

Inputs

  • Depth: pyepi_results/count_matrix.h5ad layer layers/raw (3,460 cells x 25,337 retained 100 kb autosomal bins, chr1-22; raw fragment counts). Pseudobulks: all 3,460 cells and WNN cluster 0 (1,286 cells, wnn_leiden_0.3 from integrated.h5mu). Exposure E = pooled raw counts of the pseudobulk (2.01e8 all; 5.12e7 cluster0). GC from var/GC.
  • Allele: aligned_matrices/HB.parquet and AT.parquet (bins x barcodes, joined by barcode name; coordinates checked against the h5ad; float32 integer-valued). AT = summed SNP-site depth, not unique fragments. HA = AT - HB. Counts are pooled over the same cells as the depth track. Bins with AT < 1 are marginalised (no evidence either way).
  • Calls for comparison / parameter estimation: karyogram_cluster_segments.tsv (pyEpi cn_call per bin, groups all and cluster0) and raw_accepted_breakpoints.tsv (AD breakpoints; chromosome-local bin index). Regions: research/2026-10-29-low-coverage/shared/regions.tsv rows with part == target (6 windows, bins within first_retained_start..last_retained_end) and four literature loci (chr9 20-24 Mb, chr8 127.0-128.5 Mb, chr19 >= 35 Mb, chr5 >= 70 Mb).
  • Not available: phase-set / SNP-level phase information (imbalance is phase-agnostic), unique-molecule allele counts, a matched normal, per-cell state calls, ground-truth copy number.

Method

Observations per retained bin b, chromosome by chromosome (pseudobulk of a set of cells):
  y_b = pooled raw fragment count, E = sum_b y_b;   k_b = HB_b, n_b = AT_b pooled over the same cells.
Hidden joint state s = (r, a):  r in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2} (relative depth x baseline),  a in {balanced, imbalanced}   -> 14 states
  loss states: r < 1;  gain states: r > 1;  (r = 1, imbalanced) = copy-neutral-imbalance state.

Depth emission     y_b | s ~ NB( mean mu_bs = E * g(GC_b) * r_s ,  var = mu + phi * mu^2 ),   phi = phi_s + tau2
  g(GC decile)  = c * median over cn_call==1 bins of (y/E) within the bin's GC decile   (estimated once per pseudobulk)
  c             = 5%-trimmed mean of (y/E)/median_dec over cn_call==1 bins (one scalar; makes r = 1 the MEAN level of base bins,
                  because NB means, not medians, are parameterised and between-bin dispersion is right-skewed)
  phi_s         = sampling dispersion: with halves A,B from C.stratified_halves(WNN labels, (20261104, 13)),
                  phi_A + phi_B = trimmed mean over bins of [ (y_A/E_A - y_B/E_B)^2 - (y/E)(1/E_A + 1/E_B) ] / (y/E)^2,
                  phi_s = phi_A * [sum_i e_i^2 / E^2]_full / [sum_i e_i^2 / E_A^2]_half   (e_i = cell totals), floored at 1e-8
  tau2          = max(0, (1.4826 MAD of (y/E)/g over cn_call==1 bins)^2 - median(1/mu) - phi_s)   (extra between-bin baseline variance)

Allele emission    k_b | balanced   ~ BetaBin(n_b, p = 0.5, rho)
                   k_b | imbalanced ~ 0.5 BetaBin(n_b, 0.5 + delta_s, rho) + 0.5 BetaBin(n_b, 0.5 - delta_s, rho)      (phase-agnostic)
                   BetaBin parameters a = p(1-rho)/rho, b = (1-p)(1-rho)/rho ; p clipped to [0.001, 0.999]
                   n_b < 1: log emission = 0 for every state (marginalised; never read as balanced)
  rho     = exp-06 estimator: median over 5 AT strata of (median[(HB-HA)^2/AT]/0.4549 - 1)/(median AT - 1), cn_call==1 bins with AT >= 20
  delta_s = a * |r-1| / (2r) (cap 0.5) for r != 1  [a clonal one-allele change giving relative depth r = copies/2]; a * 0.25 for r = 1 (copy-neutral imbalance, arbitrary)
  a       = mean over MYC and 5q13-qter of  delta_eff / ( |r_obs-1| / (2 r_obs) ),  r_obs = observed depth ratio in the locus, delta_eff as in exp 06

Transitions   P(s_t = j | s_{t-1} = i) = (1 - p_t) 1[i = j] + p_t * pi_j ,   p_t = min( 1 - exp(-0.01 * gap_bp / 1e5), 0.5 ),  gap_bp = start_t - start_{t-1}
  (adjacent 100 kb bins: p = 0.010; a 1 Mb QC gap: 0.095; a 7 Mb gap: 0.5)    first bin of a chromosome: p = 0.5 from pi
  pi = 0.55 on (r=1, balanced), 0.05 on (r=1, imbalanced), 0.40 shared equally by the other 12 states.   Hazard and pi are prespecified, not tuned.
Inference   log-space forward-backward (posterior per bin) and Viterbi (maximum path), independently per chromosome. Deterministic.
Region / segment summary: mean of bin posteriors; insufficient evidence = max joint-state probability of that mean < 0.8, or no bin with AT >= 1.

Seeds: only the stratified half split is random, np.random.default_rng((20261104, 13)). The HMM is deterministic. Variants: full; no_baseline_var (tau2 = 0, sampling-only NB); no_allele (allele emission removed); extra checks baseline_all_bins (decile medians over all bins instead of cn_call==1 bins) and baseline_median_only (c = 1).

Sensitivity figure

Viterbi log2 relative depth on chr7 and chr9 (all cells) for the full model, without the baseline-variance term and without the allele layer. The sampling-only model chases bin-to-bin noise; dropping the allele layer changes a minority of Viterbi depth levels (see the genome-wide table).

Limits and alternative explanations

  • Relative states, not CN. The r grid is prespecified, accessibility and dosage are confounded, and the baseline (GC-decile level of pyEpi cn_call==1 bins) is itself circular. A region the HMM calls r = 1 can sit at any copy number the pseudobulk baseline happens to share.
  • Baseline variance is the main driver and is poorly understood. A relative SD near 0.5 per bin after GC adjustment indicates mappability/accessibility structure that is not modelled. It was treated as independent per bin although it is regional, which makes posteriors overconfident where baseline structure is shared across neighbouring bins; a state that fits a regional baseline offset (for example a mappability-driven offset in 19q or 9p) is indistinguishable from copy-level change in this model.
  • AT is not independent of depth. It sums SNP-site depth, so allele evidence rises and falls with the same accessibility; it is not an independent confirmation, and an AT-poor region (9p21) gives no allele evidence at all.
  • Allelic null. exp 06 found about 31% of uncalled random windows exceed the balanced beta-binomial; here that appears as imbalance mass in some base-level regions. rho and the attenuation factor a come from the same data (two gain loci), a is applied to losses by extrapolation, and the copy-neutral-imbalance delta (0.25 nominal) is arbitrary.
  • Not tuned or calibrated. Hazard, pi, grid and the 0.8 flag threshold are fixed by hand. Posterior probabilities describe this model only. No simulation recovery study was run, so false-positive rates of the HMM are unknown (the cn_call==1 diagnostics above are not error rates because cn_call==1 is not truth).
  • Pooled all-cell and cluster0 pseudobulks dilute subclonal events; cluster0 half-split dispersion was negative (floored), so its sampling-only variant is effectively Poisson.

What the evidence supports

Supports

  • Large chr7 windows have most posterior mass on loss-level states in both pseudobulks (all: 0.92, 0.97, 1.00, 0.84) with little imbalance mass, consistent with pyEpi and with the AD structure.
  • MYC is called gain-level with imbalance by both layers (gain mass 1.00, imbalance mass 1.00), consistent with exp 06's strong allelic excess there.
  • AD breakpoints are mostly (about 56% within 2 bins) matched by an HMM depth boundary, far above a random-placement reference.
  • A between-bin baseline-variance term is necessary: sampling-only NB fragments the genome into thousands of runs.

Does not support / cannot tell

  • That the relative depth levels (0.25, 0.5, ...) are copy numbers; r = 0.25 states in chr7 mean only 'lower than the baseline by a large factor'.
  • Any claim about 9p21, 19q13-qter or 5q13-qter at confident posterior: all are flagged insufficient evidence in the full model (5q has moderate gain mass, about 0.6, split between r = 1.5 and 2 and between balanced and imbalanced states).
  • That the allele layer adds independent confirmation: it is built from the same SNP-site depth and is silent where AT is low.
  • That HMM boundaries finer than the AD breakpoints are real; most HMM depth boundaries have no AD breakpoint within 5 bins and may follow baseline structure.
  • Calibrated probabilities or error rates of any kind.

Decision impact

This supports keeping the recommendation's position that depth segmentation should be paired with a separately reported allelic evidence label and an abstention rule, and adds a quantified caveat: a joint model's value here comes mostly from (a) treating missing alleles as no evidence, (b) abstaining when the posterior is split, and (c) an explicit between-bin baseline-variance term. It does not justify replacing the AD scan with an HMM as production behaviour: the HMM cannot be validated without a recovery simulation, and its calls depend on a baseline whose regional structure is unmodelled. Suggested wording for recommendation.md: 'a joint depth+allele HMM is a useful exploratory abstention layer; adopting it requires baseline-variance modelling and a simulation-based calibration first.'

Next checks

  • Simulation recovery: plant relative depth steps and allele imbalance into the empirical baseline (resampled uncalled blocks) and measure state recovery, boundary error and false-state rate under this exact model.
  • Replace the independent per-bin baseline variance with an autocorrelated (regional) baseline or an external reference (matched normal / mappability track) and rerun; check whether the r = 1.25 'gain' excess over pyEpi neutral bins shrinks.
  • Replace the fixed r grid by a hierarchical level prior and compare the posterior at 5q and 19q.
  • Test sensitivity to the transition hazard (0.003, 0.03) and to pi, which were fixed by hand.
  • Use SNP-level data with phase blocks and unique-molecule counts, if obtainable, to replace the phase-agnostic mixture.
